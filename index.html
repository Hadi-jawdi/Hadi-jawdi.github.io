A Message From Our Headmaster

Welcome to Modabber.

We started this school with one conviction: Afghan students deserve teachers who push them as hard as any school in the world would. Every family who walks through our doors becomes part of that effort.

"We built Modabber on a simple belief: that a student here, given rigorous teachers and a real chance, can compete anywhere on earth. Every scholarship on our record started with a student we pushed to ask one harder question."
RN
Mr. Mohmmad Baqir modabber
Manager, Modabber Private High School

Campus & Student Life

More than a classroom.

Our campus is built so students have somewhere purposeful to go before the first bell and after the last one — a lab to finish an experiment, a court to play a match, a quiet room to read.

Science Laboratories

Fully equipped physics, chemistry, and biology labs for hands-on coursework.

Library & Reading Hall

A quiet study space stocked with Dari, Pashto, and English titles.

Computer & Robotics Lab

Where our robotics and coding clubs meet most weekday afternoons.

Sports Ground

Volleyball, cricket, and football teams that compete across Ghazni schools.
